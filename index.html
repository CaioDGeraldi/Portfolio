01 / CENTRAL IDEA

FRACTAW / PRODUCT VISION

Software that fits your business, not the other way around.

Companies often pay for software features they never use, while still relying on disconnected tools. Fractaw aims to change that with a modular management platform.

The approach is to let each business select the capabilities it needs and adapt workflows to its reality, keeping shared data and services connected instead of forcing a one-size-fits-all system.

02 / ENGINEERING CASE

RETORNA / COLLABORATIVE PROJECT

A loyalty program shaped around each business.

Retorna lets businesses shape their own loyalty programs, with configurable points, rewards and campaigns instead of a fixed, one-size-fits-all model.

Companies can tailor loyalty rules across their stores, including local overrides and temporary campaigns, and integrate existing sales tools through an API. Reliable transactions support that flexibility.
