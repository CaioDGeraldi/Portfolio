WINNING PROJECT / PUBLIC INNOVATION / 2026

Centro em Movimento.

A proposal to strengthen the commercial centre of Araras by connecting digital discovery to in-person visits.

Centro em Movimento is the broader revitalization initiative. Vem pro Centro is its digital platform, designed to help people discover local stores, products, services and activities before visiting the city centre — and to find reasons to explore more than one destination once they arrive.

01 / CONTEXT

The shopping journey has a digital layer

Consumers increasingly use the Internet to discover and compare products before deciding where to buy. Small businesses do not always have the time or reach to keep their catalogues visible across several separate channels. A store's social-media presence does not automatically help someone discover a business they have never heard of, or connect that discovery to nearby stores and activities.

National data helps describe this shift in behaviour. It does not prove that online shopping caused commercial decline in Araras. Local conditions such as vacant properties, business closures and footfall still require field research and a local baseline.

02 / PROPOSAL

One initiative, one connecting platform

The central idea is not simply to move local stores online. It is to use digital discovery to create reasons to visit, explore and return to the physical city centre.

01 / DISCOVERY

Digital storefront

Search stores, products and services, with prices, images, public contact details and availability declared by each business.

02 / CIRCULATION

Discovery routes and calendar

Find themed routes, activities and events that encourage people to explore different parts of the centre, not just one destination.

03 / RETURN VISITS

Shared loyalty

Cross-store campaigns can give customers a reason to visit another participating business, with clear eligibility and a defined sponsor for each benefit.

03 / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

From discovery to another visit

  1. 01 / DISCOVER

    Find a store, product or activity

    Search the storefront or check the local calendar before deciding where to go.

  2. 02 / VISIT

    Plan the physical visit

    Store details and contact channels help people decide where to go. A reservation request opens an external conversation, such as WhatsApp.

  3. 03 / EXPLORE

    Discover another part of the centre

    Themed routes and nearby activities introduce businesses people had not planned to find.

  4. 04 / CONSUME

    Purchase in the store

    The commercial transaction remains in person. Participating businesses can confirm an eligible purchase for a campaign.

  5. 05 / RETURN

    Keep a reason to come back

    Shared campaigns and new activities can encourage another visit.

05 / IMPLEMENTATION

What the prototype does — and does not — claim

Current MVP direction

The current MVP is a responsive web application focused on discovery, public business information, store-managed catalogues, routes, a local calendar and campaign mechanics. Merchants are responsible for declared prices and availability.

Reservation requests are sent through external contact channels. Purchases remain in the physical store; the merchant explicitly records an eligible purchase through the owner flow. A QR identifies the consumer account, but does not prove payment, physical presence or a point-of-sale transaction.

Outside current scope

No online checkout, payment processing, delivery logistics, real-time stock or point-of-sale integration is claimed. A search with no results is not proof that a product or service does not exist in Araras. Institutional analytics require additional privacy controls, local validation and later implementation.

More visibility, visits, discovery across stores and repeat visits are intended outcomes, not established results. They need to be evaluated in a local pilot with participating businesses and a baseline. The project does not claim measured growth in revenue, footfall or store occupancy.

06 / RECOGNITION & PARTNERSHIPS

A winning proposal, with implementation still ahead

Centro em Movimento was a winning project at the 1st Inova Araras 2026, held on 19 November 2026.

The proposal identifies potential institutional partners, including SEBRAE, the Municipality of Araras and the Government of the State of São Paulo. These are proposed relationships, not confirmed partnerships, funding or formal commitments.
