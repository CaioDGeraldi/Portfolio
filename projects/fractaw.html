PRODUCT VISION · MODULAR BUSINESS SOFTWARE

Fractaw.

Business management without disconnected workflows.

Fractaw addresses the difficulty of running a business across unrelated tools. It proposes a shared management foundation with modules chosen for the organization's actual work, so information and processes can stay connected without forcing different industries into the same workflow.

01 / CONTEXT

The problem

Consider a school and a beverage distributor. Both need to manage operations, people and information, but their daily workflows are different. A single rigid system fits neither one particularly well.

Switching between separate tools also costs attention: information has to be entered twice, processes become inconsistent and teams must learn how everything fits together.

Fractaw's central question is simple: can software share a stable foundation while allowing the business-specific parts to change?

02 / PRODUCT

The approach

The approach combines shared organizational services with independent business modules. A school and a beverage distributor can rely on a common foundation while keeping their daily operations different.

03 / PRINCIPLES

Product decisions

01

Start with the workflow

Understand what a team needs to accomplish before deciding which screens or technical modules to build.

02

Keep modules independent

Different business capabilities should have understandable boundaries and explicit dependencies.

03

Build for change

The goal is to evolve shared behavior without forcing every type of company into the same process.

Current scope
